# NIBM Certified ICT Teacher: Essentials (NCIT-E)

**Version:** v0.1 · **Date:** 26 November 2026  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Programme Lead  
**Source:** NCIT KT Kit v3.4

Candidate materials for NCIT-E, the Essentials level of the NIBM Certified ICT Teacher
programme, School of Computing & Engineering, National Institute of Business Management.

Materials are posted unit by unit, once each unit pack has been reviewed and released.

## What an NCIT-E holder can do

> *An NCIT-E holder can plan, teach, and assess ICT lessons, including in classrooms
> without a functioning computer laboratory, and can identify and address the
> misconceptions learners commonly form about ICT concepts. Competence was evidenced
> at the grade band recorded on the transcript.*

## The award

| | |
|---|---|
| Credit | 3 SLQF credits (150 notional hours) |
| Volume | 60 guided hours (45 contact, 15 practical) and 90 independent study hours |
| Sessions | 20 guided sessions of 3 hours: 15 contact, 5 practical |
| Duration | 15 teaching weeks |
| Grade band | Grade 6 to A/L. You complete A2 and A3 in your own teaching band |
| Composition | 43% pedagogy, 32% ICT content, 25% policy, assessment, safeguarding and professional practice |
| Delivery language | English |
| Standards | Designed in alignment with the UNESCO ICT Competency Framework for Teachers (v3). Mapped to the ISTE Standards for Educators |
