WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The pathway is one subject over eight grades in three stages, so every topic you teach has a before and an after. The bands are not on the same footing during the reform transition: Every band has a recorded version, but at Ordinary Level a newer version has been reported and not yet confirmed. That is why the programme never says "the current syllabus" without naming the document and its year, and why the version check comes before any lesson plan.
