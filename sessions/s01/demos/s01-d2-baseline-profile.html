WHAT YOU JUST SAW

A profile is only as good as its evidence. A rating with something you did behind it is a fact you can build on; a rating without one is a guess, which is why the tool flags ratings of 3 or 4 with no evidence. The shape shows where your second competence is still forming, and the unit list shows where in the programme that work happens. Keep the profile: you will compare it with your practice at the end of the programme.
