WHAT AN NCIT-E HOLDER CAN DO

An NCIT-E holder can plan, teach, and assess ICT lessons, including in classrooms without a functioning computer laboratory, and can identify and address the misconceptions learners commonly form about ICT concepts. Competence was evidenced at the grade band recorded on the transcript.

This portal holds your materials for NCIT-E, unit by unit. Each session has notes you can study on your own, the activities used in class, and the independent study that follows it. Independent study is 90 of the award's 150 notional hours, so the notes are written to be used between sessions, not only during them.

  • 150notional hours: 60 guided (45 contact, 15 practical) and 90 independent study. 3 SLQF credits
  • 20 sessionsof 3 hours over 15 teaching weeks: 15 contact sessions and 5 practical sessions
  • 16 of 20Attend at least 16 of 20 sessions (80%). Practical sessions missed require a documented make-up.

Assessment window

Teaching weeks 16 and 17. The Board of Examiners follows.

Three components: an objective test (A1), a lesson package (A2) and a micro-teach (A3). See Assessment.

Designed in alignment with the UNESCO ICT Competency Framework for Teachers (v3). Mapped to the ISTE Standards for Educators. Composition: 43% pedagogy, 32% ICT content, 25% policy, assessment, safeguarding and professional practice.
